About me

A bit about
who I am

I’m a final-year student doctor in Melbourne with a passion for teaching, debating, travel, and photography. Occasionally, I like to build things for the web. I care about education, good design, and finding the interesting in the ordinary.

Outside the hospital, you’ll find me tutoring VCE students, coaching competitive debaters, or chasing good light with a camera. I’m drawn to the spaces where disciplines overlap: medicine and communication, technology and empathy, craft and clarity.

I’ve had the privilege of working with an independent film studio and a technology start-up, and I’m always open to interesting conversations.
